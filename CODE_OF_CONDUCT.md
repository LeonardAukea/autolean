# Code of conduct

AutoLean is a place for exact, patient work on mathematics and software.
Participants are expected to:

- discuss claims through evidence and reproducible examples;
- distinguish mistakes from misconduct;
- critique work without belittling its author;
- respect privacy, attribution, and publication boundaries;
- make room for questions from people learning the system.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
accepted in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns to `leonard.aukea@skygrid.ai`. Include the relevant
links or records and the outcome needed for safety. Reports are handled
privately, with access limited to the people required to investigate them.
Enforcement may include a warning, removal of content, a temporary restriction,
or a permanent ban. Decisions consider the evidence, impact, pattern, and
repair offered.
